("Provider"). **Effective date:** `[DATE]`.

## 1. What this covers
When a person using Lunee ("Patient") chooses to send the Provider a request, Lunee may show the
Provider information the Patient explicitly selected: their first name, and optionally their health
profile and specific lab reports ("Shared Data"). The Provider receives Shared Data only to answer
that request (perform a test, hold an appointment, call back, or give advice).

## 2. Provider's duties
1. Use Shared Data only for the Patient's request and for delivering the care they asked for.
2. Do not sell, share, publish or use Shared Data for marketing, research or model training.
3. Do not copy Shared Data into other systems except the Provider's clinical records for that
   Patient, which the Provider keeps under its own legal duties as a healthcare provider.
4. Keep Shared Data confidential; limit access to staff who need it; use unique staff accounts.
5. Protect it with reasonable security (encryption on devices, screen locks, no shared logins).
6. Tell Lunee within `[24/72]` hours of learning of any breach affecting Shared Data.
7. Respect withdrawal: when a Patient cancels a request or the Provider declines it, the Provider
   must stop using the Shared Data for that request. Lunee removes access immediately.
8. Handle Patient requests (access, correction, deletion) that Lunee forwards, within `[30]` days.
9. Do not contact Patients outside the Lunee request unless the Patient has separately agreed.

## 3. Lunee's duties
1. Show the Patient exactly what will be shared and record their consent before anything is shared.
2. Share only what the Patient selected. Never share another person's data.
3. Remove Provider access when the Patient cancels or the Provider declines.
4. Keep the Provider's listing accurate and remove it on request.

## 5. Accuracy and no medical advice from Lunee
Lunee provides general information and suggestions. Clinical decisions are the Provider's alone.

## 6. Term and ending
Either party may end this agreement on `[30]` days' notice, or immediately for a breach. On ending,
the Provider's listing is removed and access to any active requests ends.
