## 1. What HERAI is — and is not

HERAI provides health **information and risk-awareness support**. It helps you track
symptoms and cycles, understand lab reports in plain language, and prepare questions for
a clinician.

**HERAI does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.** It is not a medical
device, not a substitute for a licensed clinician, and not a triage or emergency service.

By using HERAI you accept that:

- Nothing in the app is a diagnosis, however confident the wording appears.
- Analysis is generated by automated systems and **can be incomplete or wrong**.
- You should not start, stop or change any treatment based on what HERAI tells you.
- You should not delay seeking medical care because of anything HERAI shows you.

**In an emergency, contact your local emergency services or go to a hospital
immediately.** HERAI may display an emergency warning, but it cannot reliably detect
emergencies and must never be relied on to do so.
